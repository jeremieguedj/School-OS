# ElevenLabs — optional audio companion

Use only when the parent selected audio and authorized this service/account for
the current brief. Apply [brief recipes](../operations/brief-recipes.md). The
agent's authorized connector owns generation, authentication and retrieval;
School-OS supplies the source-grounded narration and delivery policy.

ElevenLabs documents generating speech from text and downloading generated audio.
That describes the product, not a particular connector's access, available
voices or billing permissions.
[Text to Speech](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/playground/text-to-speech)
and [audio retrieval](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/core-capabilities/text-to-speech/how-do-i-download-generated-files-from-text-to-speech).

## Apply the meaning

1. Compose the same verified information selected for the written brief. Keep
   qualifications, school dates, open tasks, awaiting-parent-confirmation tasks
   and material freshness/synchronization limits in the narration. Do not turn
   the audio script into a different factual brief.
2. Use the parent's selected voice/language preferences where actually available.
   Do not silently choose a different voice, clone a voice, add a subscription
   or invent service permissions. Resolve a missing choice only when needed.
3. Ask the authorized connector to generate audio under its supported limits.
   Record the generating service/agent and actual observation in the operation
   report. A request receipt is not proof of an available audio file.
4. Retrieve the generated artifact through the authorized route. Establish that
   the complete artifact corresponds to this narration and is usable by the
   selected delivery route. If available, inspect/listen to it within the
   authorized operation; do not claim content verification from a filename.
5. Deliver configured audio together with the authorized email. A verified audio
   failure produces email alone with a clear failure notice. An uncertain
   generation/retrieval result needs verification before any repeat; do not
   classify a generic error as a failed generation or blindly incur another one.
6. After verified delivery, discard accessible temporary audio. Store no canonical
   audio archive on Drive. Do not claim deletion of provider-managed history or
   the parent's delivered copy; this mapping grants no provider-history deletion.

A route that generates speech but cannot retrieve a deliverable artifact is
insufficient for this flow. A route lacking verification exposes that uncertainty.
An unavailable service does not authorize another vendor automatically. No fixed
SDK, model, endpoint, retry policy, dedicated machine or background worker is
required or selected here. No live generation or delivery has been qualified.
