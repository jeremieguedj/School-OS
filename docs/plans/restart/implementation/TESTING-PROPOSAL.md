- Use independent fictional logical labels, not provider-entry counts. Report

  semantic knowledge, tasks and output status separately. Metadata association
  cannot establish content equality or transfer unread coverage. Daily success
  cannot erase a historical gap; any relevant unresolved gap blocks the ordinary
  daily brief under the new gate.
- Grade substantive facts, conditions, exceptions, provenance and action
  disposition. Preserve explicit correction relationships and unresolved
  contradictions. D5 does not make the newest timestamp an automatic winner.
  Parent plans/completion remain separate from school deadlines.
- For recurring tasks, preserve the entire source requirement, applicability,
  exceptions and occurrence history. Use supported native recurrence or D5's
  next-14-day occurrence fallback with continuation; one completion does not
  complete the series. Do not treat advisory guidance as a required action.
- Compare last shared, current canonical and current task-app values by field.
  Merge compatible separate-field changes; retain divergent same-field edits
  for review. Missing app entries do not prove canonical cancellation/completion.
- Grade queries using verified knowledge and the coverage actually examined.
  Unread or unexamined material is not irrelevant merely because there are no
  query hits. Report known facts with limits when coverage cannot establish an
  exhaustive answer. Do not reveal a hidden fixture's contents to explain a gap.
- Inspect normal readback for every required saved result. A source-index save
  does not prove knowledge/tasks/coverage also saved. Do not grade the deferred
  canonical-write repair mechanism as present or missing MVP functionality.
- For uncertain external actions, inspect the agent's actual authorized
  validation and evidence. A generic error establishes neither success nor
  failure, throttling nor safe retry. If uncertainty remains, report it rather
  than claim success or repeat blindly. Do not require a new central ledger,

  a later semantic trial, bind expectations to its exact packet and ordered

  managed-agent qualification separate. Vendor names or declared fixture sizes
  do not establish observed capability or capacity.

## New written acceptance guidance

**S32 — more work than the former caps.** The fictional workload contains 120
independently labeled logical emails, with relevant information after positions
25 and 100, plus a document larger than the former 8 MiB ceiling readable through
an actually capable route. The agent selects safe resource chunks and follows
continuations within the same logical run. Neither the old numbers nor a short
page establishes completion. It finishes all relevant input and verifies saved
results before the ordinary daily brief. No School-OS batch scheduler, fixed
message quota or automatic later-job creation is accepted as the implementation.
An actual inability to finish is a blocker, not a pass. These quantities are
authored stimuli, not measured throughput or provider-entry ground truth.

**S33 — ingestion cannot finish.** A relevant message or attachment cannot be
read with the available authorized routes, or complete discovery cannot be
established. Preserve the known coverage and report the blocker through an
authorized surface. Do not compose/send the ordinary daily brief as if its
ingestion were complete, or bypass the gate with its audio version. Do not create
a new notification channel or scheduled retry. A separately requested question
may still receive a coverage-qualified answer under the approved query rule.
After an authorized capable route becomes available, reassess actual completion
before ordinary briefing. The fixture does not guarantee such a route exists.
