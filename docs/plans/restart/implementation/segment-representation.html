When one saved item is too large for a file

School-OS can add pages for more records. This proposal covers the different case where one permitted field is too large to fit on a page by itself.

Start with the actual problem

School-OS stores processed knowledge and tasks in many bounded JSON files on Google Drive. JSON is field-labelled structured text. A Knowledge record, for example, has a statement field, fields saying who or what it applies to, and fields linking it to its source. It is not a copy of the raw email or PDF.

School-OS chose a default limit of 64 KiB, exactly 65,536 encoded bytes, for each JSON page. Field names, punctuation, escaped characters and the page's surrounding structure all count. This is a School-OS resource boundary. It is not an inherent JSON or Google Drive limit, and it does not cap the family's whole history.

A realistic case is a long, extracted school guideline whose conditions, exceptions and qualifications must be retained. This is the processed guideline, not a raw-source archive. Two histories can also grow: the evidence-backed links among corrected, supported, replaced or conflicting Knowledge, and the reviews of evidence that a Task may be complete. The proposal does not claim that ordinary school messages are all huge or that tests have established how often overflow happens.

Four useful definitions. Knowledge is one source-linked piece of retained information at its real child, family or school scope, not the entire year in one record. A Task is one action that may need completing. A relationship links two Knowledge records based on evidence. A completion review records evidence that a Task may be finished and the parent's response; evidence alone does not close the Task.

The short statements elsewhere in the documentation are artificial teaching examples. The approved design already requires meaningful information to remain lossless and says an oversized value should use numbered pieces. It never defined the exact owner, links, order, expected count or content format for those pieces. One GPT Work session could therefore improvise a shape that a later Gemini session cannot reliably read. We missed this representation detail. The broader architecture is not being reopened, but the user's architecture rule requires explicit approval of this exact shape.

Recommendation: one shared linked-piece format

Keep the original Knowledge or Task record as the owner. It keeps its ID, scope, source, dates and other ordinary fields. Where the oversized value would have been, store a small pointer to the first piece and the number of pieces expected.

Each piece says which owner and field it belongs to, its numbered position, its part of the text and, except on the last piece, which piece comes next. Every piece is stored on an ordinary bounded page.

The only permitted fields are:

  1. Knowledge statement: the substantive information and qualifications;
  2. Knowledge relationships: evidence-backed links to other Knowledge; and
  3. Task parent_state.completion_reviews: the completion-evidence review history.

Small values keep their existing form. All other non-oversized data stays unchanged. Pieces are storage belonging to the owner; they are not new Knowledge, Tasks, emails or source material.

A fictional field-trip guideline

Suppose fictional Pine School publishes a detailed field-trip guideline. The Knowledge record says it applies at school scope and links to the original source. Its extracted statement preserves transport rules, medical exceptions and conditional pickup instructions. After JSON encoding, that one statement would make its page exceed 64 KiB.

To answer a question, a reader finds the Knowledge record through the approved index, follows the first pointer and checks that all three distinct pieces have the right owner, field and positions. It joins the text and decodes it once to recover the complete statement, then answers with its source. If S2 is missing, the statement is incomplete. The reader must not treat S1 and S3 as a shorter guideline.

The same process reconstructs either permitted history. The semantic index points to the owning Knowledge or Task; it does not present each piece as an independent result.

Editing means building a new chain first

Build.
Create a complete new chain with new School-OS IDs.
Verify.
Save and read back the pieces and their normal location entries.
Switch.
Change and read back the owner pointer, revision and required indexes.

This order reduces the chance that the owner points at pieces that were never saved. It is not a transaction and promises no recovery from an interrupted multi-file write. Old chains may remain unreachable after edits and consume storage. No automatic cleanup, repair service, checksum identity or generic write engine is added.

What this provides, and what remains unknown

Benefit: fresh agents get one provider-independent way to reconstruct the complete permitted value while every page and the owner's pointer stay bounded.

Cost: oversized values require extra Drive reads and writes. Replacing one copies its chain, old pieces may remain, and individual JSON-text chunks are awkward to inspect alone.

Only the reconstructed value has its original string or array meaning. We do not yet know the real frequency of overflow or the connector cost and reliability; later authorized trials may observe those. This format does not promise that every agent environment can process an arbitrarily large single field.

Alternatives considered

  • Separate piece formats for each field. Pieces could be more specific, but agents would need to implement three parallel formats consistently.
  • Raise the page limit or allow occasional large pages. This reduces reads but changes the approved resource bounds and may exceed managed-agent limits.
  • Keep blocking oversized values. This avoids a new shape, but an affected email cannot be fully ingested without discarding required information.

The exact approval requested

For an oversized statement or either of the two histories listed above, use the linked, numbered pieces shown here, verify that the complete set is present, and write a new set when the value changes.

Approval would define the format. It would not qualify performance, promise interrupted-write recovery or automatic cleanup, authorize another field, or start any trial.

Technical reference: exact proposed JSON and field dictionary

For a segmented Knowledge statement, the existing segment_refs field contains:

{
  "value_encoding": "json_text",
  "first_ref": {"family": "record_segment", "id": "segment_<uuid-v4>"},
  "segment_count": 2
}

For oversized relationships or parent_state.completion_reviews, replace the array with {"state":"segmented","segment_refs": <the same descriptor>}. This is a known segmented value, not an empty or unknown array.

A segment record, inside the normal D1 page envelope, is:

{
  "segment_id": "segment_<uuid-v4>",
  "owner_ref": {"family": "knowledge", "id": "knowledge_<uuid-v4>"},
  "field_path": "statement",
  "ordinal": 1,
  "text_chunk": "first part of the JSON-encoded value",
  "next_ref": {"family": "record_segment", "id": "segment_<another-uuid-v4>"}
}
FieldMeaning
segment_idSchool-OS ID assigned once, never derived from content.
owner_refThe owning Knowledge or Task.
field_pathExactly one of the three permitted fields, valid for that owner family.
ordinalOne-based position.
text_chunkOne lossless portion of the whole JSON-encoded value.
next_refNext segment; omitted on the last.
first_refThe owner's pointer to the first segment.
segment_countThe exact number of distinct segments expected.
value_encodingjson_text; decode once after joining all chunks.

Encode a statement as a JSON string and either history as a JSON array. Split losslessly at character boundaries, preferring paragraphs where possible. Every fully encoded containing page, including escaping and envelope overhead, remains at or below 64 KiB. Locate segments through the approved bounded family directory and locator mechanism.

A reader verifies owner, field, ordinal, distinct IDs, links and exact count before joining and decoding to the original type. A missing piece, cycle, duplicate ordinal, wrong owner, wrong type or unexpected continuation means incomplete data. Indexes use the reconstructed whole value. Whole-email ingestion cannot complete until all pieces are saved and checked. Verified segments are immutable; edits go through the owner by writing a new verified chain.
